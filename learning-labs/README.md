## Promotion rule

Completing a lab does not automatically make it a portfolio case study.

Promote a lab into `case-studies/` only when it demonstrates a defensible investigative workflow with:

- a clear objective or investigative question
- evidence sources and provenance
- a timeline or event sequence
- analysis and investigative pivots
- findings and limitations
- response or remediation considerations
- detection opportunities where relevant
- concise lessons learned

Keep challenge flags, credentials, secrets, and unnecessary answer-key material out of public notes.
